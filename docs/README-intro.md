# Apple SAT

**A little direction for your next SAT.**

Apple SAT is a free, open-source desktop study app for **Mac and Windows**. Start with your official SAT or Bluebook results, follow a daily study path, and keep track of what you can do independently.

[Download Apple SAT](https://bobananaa.github.io/apple-study-sat/) · [Latest release](https://github.com/boBananaa/apple-study-sat/releases/latest) · [Report an issue](https://github.com/boBananaa/apple-study-sat/issues)

![Apple SAT daily study path](screenshots/roadmap.png)

## A study routine you can follow

- **Start with your real results.** Enter your official results, goal, and test date. The roadmap prioritizes skills using the evidence you provide and your later practice.
- **Learn before guessing again.** Original lessons cover Math and Reading and Writing, with worked examples and short checks.
- **Practice the Desmos setup.** Explore 32 calculator methods, each with an original application question, an optional hint, and an explanation after your answer.
- **Keep the first attempt honest.** Corrections, help used, and later checks remain separate from your original answer. A retry does not erase a mistake.
- **Return to official checkpoints.** Take Bluebook practice tests periodically and enter your new results. App practice is not a calibrated SAT score or a score guarantee.

## Download

Get the latest **universal Mac app** for Apple silicon and Intel or the **Windows installer** from the [download page](https://bobananaa.github.io/apple-study-sat/). If a platform’s installer has not been published, the page links to available releases instead.

These early preview builds are not yet Apple-notarized or Windows publisher-signed. Your operating system may show a warning. The desktop app checks for updates automatically and verifies update signatures before installation; update signing is separate from platform code signing.

## What comes with the app

Original lessons, Desmos guides and application practice, vocabulary, planning, and study tools are included. **Official question-bank material is not bundled.** Import your own supported question bank to use official question practice and assembled assessments. The software license does not grant rights to third-party question material.

Progress stays on your device. Export backups to keep a copy or move to another computer. No account, paid subscription, or AI API key is required. The app uses authored content and supplied answer keys; it does not send your work to a live AI tutor.
