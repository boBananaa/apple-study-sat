Start with where
you actually are.

An official score report gives your plan a starting point. Your practice helps decide what comes next.

  1. Bring your results

    Enter your official SAT or Bluebook results, your goal, and your test date. Apple SAT uses the evidence you provide to prioritize your study.

  2. Follow today’s path

    Move from a focused lesson to practice, corrections, and later checks. Your first answers stay intact, so a retry doesn’t rewrite your progress.

  3. Check in with Bluebook

    Take official practice tests periodically and add the new results. Your roadmap can change as your needs do.

A lesson before
another guess.

Find original lessons for Math and Reading and Writing, with worked examples and short checks to put the idea into practice.

Once your accuracy and reasoning are stable, try Question Rush: short timed rounds with pace choices, streaks, and speed stars. Both SAT sections are supported.

When you miss a question, work through the setup and the reason for the mistake. Return later to check what you remember.

Explore 32 calculator methods, each with its own original application question. Plan your setup, ask for a small hint if you need one, then check the answer and explanation.

Your study space.
On your computer.

Apple SAT is free and open source. Your study progress stays on your device, with exports for backups or moving to another computer. No account or AI API key is required.

Lessons and original Desmos practice come with the app. Official question-bank material does not. Import your own supported question bank to use official question practice and assembled assessments.
